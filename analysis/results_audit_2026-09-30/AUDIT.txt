RESULTS AND CODE AUDIT — 30 SEPTEMBER 2026

Conclusion
----------
The current evidence does not establish a reproduction of the paper's results,
or a causal SoftRole architecture advantage. A newly identified active bug in
the released HetNet sensory extractor discards most target indicators. A known
FC simulator bug affects all three architectures. SoftRole's audited learner
and episode accounting passed the checks below, but the experiment as a whole
cannot be certified bug-free, and FC cannot be treated as validated physical
firefighting. No runtime code, training settings, existing inputs/checkpoints,
or cluster jobs were changed in this audit.

2. Confirmed major legacy sensory-extraction defect (new finding)
----------------------------------------------------------------
File: hetgat/uavnet.py:204-219, called unconditionally by forward at 300-327.
Each raw observation cell contains 25 position entries plus four sensory
entries. get_obs_features advances by P_s=25 instead of the full stride 29.
Expected sensory slices: 25:29,54:58,83:87,...
Actual sensory slices:   25:29,50:54,75:79,...

Independent tagged-cell and target-impulse probes using the actual methods:
* PP/PCP: 96/100 extracted slots per sensing agent have the wrong schema index;
  only 16/100 come from any sensory channel. Target indicators survive only
  in four of 25 relative cells: indices 0, 6, 12, 19, corresponding to offsets
  (-2,-2),(-1,-1),(0,0),(+1,+2).
* FC: 32/36 slots have the wrong index; only 8/36 come from sensory channels.
  Target indicators survive only in two of 9 cells: indices 0, 6, corresponding
  to offsets (-1,-1),(+1,-1).
* The separate position extractor correctly drops ALL target-channel impulses;
  it provides no alternate route for discarded target indicators. Retained
  indicators in other agents' observations and history can still help.

This is not simply an invertible permutation. It removes sensory information.
All recorded reproduction configurations use this active non-tensor A2C path.
The downloaded current upstream uavnet.py is byte-identical to local, SHA256
343e1df3337723d1323d687e4c94d48b31011ab8890fbd8f196d948d02ff5f55.
Upstream: https://github.com/CORE-Robotics-Lab/HetNet/blob/main/hetgat/uavnet.py
It is a concrete confound and plausible contributor to poor learning, including
PP. Its share of the performance gap requires controlled retraining; this audit
does not identify the code revision actually used for the published results.

Evidence: legacy/probe_features.py, feature_findings.json, upstream_uavnet.py.
An independent reviewer reproduced the retained target-cell sets and verified
the actual runtime call chain and absence of a position-branch bypass.

3. FC is still physically defective for all three architectures
-------------------------------------------------------------
WildFire_Simulate_Original.py:172 allocates zero-filled propagation rows. Line 178
skips fronts on the top or left boundary; line 227 returns unwritten rows.
fire_commander_env.py:280-293 converts these placeholders into fires at (0,0).
Its source-capture path at 401-426 and reward at 509-510 can then award +10.

Fresh exhaustive initial-cell probe: eight of 25 initial fire locations create a
nonexistent origin fire after one stay step. In a deterministic case with the
real fire at (0,2), extinguishing phantom (0,0) returns [-0.1,-0.1,+10], team +9.8,
while the real fire remains. It also removes the propagation front. The current
physics files match all six archived SoftRole FC runs byte-for-byte. Legacy
HetNet uses the same physical simulator. Final-real-fire capture DOES terminate
and report success correctly in the separate sanity check.

FC learning is also unstable across seeds. Latest shared seed successes are
95.15%,46.22%,43.12%; banked 69.92%,15.53%,19.90%. At a common 33.5M-step window,
shared/banked mean success is 63.91%/32.97%, with length 178.35/233.31. A persistent
simulator defect does not by itself explain the timing of late regressions.
The earlier saved FC audit also found a badly inaccurate terminal critic at
one checkpoint; that is prior evidence of learned value error, not a newly
demonstrated GAE arithmetic defect or an established cause of later collapse.

Evidence: fc/confirm_fc.py and fc/findings.json. Further historical diagnostics
are in analysis/fc_audit_2026-09-30/ and are not claimed as rerun here.

4. Why SoftRole can look much better without proving a gate advantage
-------------------------------------------------------------------
SoftRole reshapes the raw cells correctly, avoiding the legacy stride defect.
It also enables independent observation copies. In the legacy view-based path,
masking a blind A agent modifies overlapping P-agent observations. On fixed
reset seeds 0-999, independently comparing identical simulator states:
* PCP: target visible in 1144 corrected P-agent views, 456 legacy views. The
  legacy path erased 688/1144 (60.14%) otherwise visible target observations.
* FC: 510 corrected versus 387 legacy visible views, erasing 123/510 (24.12%).
* PP: no copy-path difference, because the team has no blind A agents.
These percentages concern this reset panel and raw views, before the separate
legacy feature-extraction defect; they are not training-trajectory estimates.
Matched actions across 90 episodes / 10,992 transitions preserved positions,
rewards, termination and info exactly between observation-copy settings.

The comparison also changes recurrence, communication, reward credit and
optimization. Legacy has typed Real communication and per-class critics;
SoftRole has untyped binary communication, action/communication recurrence and
a pooled team critic. Both SoftRole variants use the same corrected inputs and
learner; shared has one transform, banked four convex experts with a learned
gate. Banked adds capacity as well as gating. At common 20M PCP samples,
shared length 7.001 beats banked 8.752. The data do not show that banks are the
reason either pipeline beats the compromised legacy reference.

5. SoftRole audit evidence and its limits
---------------------------------------
The current full suite passed: 178 tests in 49.14 seconds. Independently checked
model/learning/rollout/aggregation: common team credit, finite-horizon GAE,
physical action masking, count-preserving observation transform, affine banks,
sender/receiver distinction, null-message handling, actor/critic separation,
episode averaging and single clipping. No new core SoftRole implementation
defect was found in these paths. Straight-through bits, approximate-critic GAE
and recurrence truncation remain declared approximations.

Additional direct probes verified that other agents' inputs cannot change a
local actor without a message path, and fresh current message noise affects
current action logits but not the pre-message critic or gate.

A prespecified nominal diagnostic panel used the latest LOCALLY ARCHIVED
seed 0 checkpoint by epoch for each task/model, plus its seeded initialization:
six checkpoint policies + six initialization policies, 12 common scenarios each,
144 episodes total, panel seed 93001. Actions were independently replayed in
fresh simulators. Every step reward, summed agent/team return, episode length,
termination and physical success condition reconciled; checkpoint byte hashes
were unchanged. Trained PP policies both succeeded 12/12, with lengths 3.92/3.75
(shared/banked), versus 6/12 and 4/12 at initialization, lengths 63.58/69.83.
Trained PCP succeeded 11/12 and 12/12 versus 3/12 and 2/12 at initialization.
This supports actual learned behavior within the supplied simulators rather
than fabricated short-episode accounting. It is a tiny nominal panel with
unequal checkpoint progress, not a model ranking or final evaluation. FC replay
agreement certifies bookkeeping within the buggy simulator, not its physics.

Model, learning, scenarios and simulator sources were compared to 18 archived
SoftRole runs; the selected files match their recorded manifests. Current
env/rollout differences add opt-in diagnostics; train differences concern source
archiving. The newest copied stdout extends archived metric prefixes exactly,
but newer checkpoint/source snapshots are absent. No claim is made to have
audited unavailable late-checkpoint tensors or live cluster state.

Evidence: softrole/{probe.py,probe_results.json,observation_probe.py,
observation_results.json} and their console records.

failures; unlimited communication range; four collectors; 10 updates/epoch;
complete-episode batch floor 500 steps per collector/update. A joint environment
step advances all agents once. Checkpoints are requested every 50 epochs/final.

7. What is needed before a defensible comparison
----------------------------------------------
Preserve these runs as released-code and original SoftRole evidence. Implement
separately versioned, opt-in corrections for the legacy sensory stride and
observation copies, with schema/visibility tests; do not silently relabel old
weights as trained on corrected inputs. Correct FC propagation/front validity,
then validate reward/discovery/termination with deterministic physical scenarios.
Retrain a corrected HetNet reference and both SoftRole variants under common
environment semantics and actual sample budgets. Use an explicit release-recipe
label versus a separate paper-specification reconstruction. Assess final frozen
policies on a locked common panel across training seeds. Corrections applied
only at evaluation introduce an input/physics distribution shift and cannot
repair the training history. Controlled ablations are required to separate the
input fix, learner changes and banks. The current evidence does not justify
spending further FC comparison compute under the known defective semantics.

---------------

runtime tests: .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q. No research training or job
submission ran.
