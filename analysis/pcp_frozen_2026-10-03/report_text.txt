2. Sensor-loss result: an exposure limitation
Only 1 of 600 policy-scenario assignments reached a sensor-loss event (shared seed 0: 1/100; the other five policies:
0/100). The other 599 ended before their own scheduled event in steps 10-30, not necessarily before step 10. All six
failure/sham pairs have identical aggregate success and returns; the sole exposed trial fails in both conditions. [1,2]
For nonzero exposure and verified pre-event coupling, unexposed outcomes match and:
 mean(failure - sham) = (exposed / assigned) x mean(exposed-pair differences)
Thus shared seed 0's success-rate effect can be at most 1 percentage point on this panel; the zero-exposure pairs never test physical sensor
loss. Full JSON traces are required to verify coupling. Exposure is policy-dependent, so exposed-only results are not a common cross-policy test
population. [3]
