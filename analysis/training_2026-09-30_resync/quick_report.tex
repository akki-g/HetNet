\subsection{Why the comparisons are defined this
way}\label{why-the-comparisons-are-defined-this-way}

For epoch \(e\), let \(n_e\) be completed episodes and \(y_e\) an
episode-mean metric. The SoftRole window statistic is

\[\widehat y_s(B)=\frac{\sum_{e\in W_s(B)}n_e y_e}{\sum_{e\in W_s(B)}n_e},
\qquad \overline y(B)=\frac13\sum_{s=0}^{2}\widehat y_s(B).\]

Expanding \(n_e y_e\) as the sum of episode outcomes proves that this
pools episodes within a run; equal seed weights prevent a faster run
from dominating the group estimate. \(W_s(B)\) is the last 50 completed
epochs ending no later than \(B\). The maximum endpoint shortfall is
17,397 steps for PP, 16,176 for PCP and 24,828 for FC, each below 0.12\%
of its budget. Window starts and sample counts are recorded in
\texttt{summary.json}; these are approximate sample-matched training
windows, not the same frozen policy or identical window widths.

For reproduction's common-epoch table, the estimand is instead
\(\overline y=\frac13\sum_s\frac1{50}\sum_{e=E-49}^{E}y_{s,e}\). Its
success values are printed to two decimals, allowing up to 0.5
percentage points of rounding error in an individual value and in a
worst-case averaged value. Reproduction's printed cumulative counters
cannot supply the missing episode weights: \texttt{main.py} adds the
within-epoch cumulative statistic after each batch. If batch \(i\)
contributes \(b_i\) steps and there are \(m\) batches,

\[\Delta C=\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{i=1}^{j}b_i
=\sum_{i=1}^{m}(m-i+1)b_i\ne\sum_{i=1}^{m}b_i.\]

The generally unequal coefficients show why neither the counter nor a
fixed division recovers the true sample count. The earlier JSON archive
has valid counts, but it does not cover the new reproduction tail.

No confidence bands, significance claims or convergence claims are
inferred from three seeds and changing-policy training data. Seed ranges
are descriptive; episodes sharing a training run are not independent
replications of training. The caution about few-run point estimates
follows \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264}{Agarwal et al., NeurIPS
2021}. Entropy, null attention and value loss are diagnostics, not
causal evidence of role discovery, communication benefit or failure
robustness. New gradient-norm records were not supplied, so the previous
clipping audit is not extended to this tail.
